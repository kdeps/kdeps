# kdeps code of conduct

kdeps and its members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make
participation in our community a positive, inclusive, and safe experience for
everyone, regardless of ability, age, background, body size or type, caste,
disability, education, ethnicity, family status, gender identity and
expression, geographic location, level of experience, nationality, native
language, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity and
orientation, or socio-economic status.

This document is adapted from the
[Good Docs Project code of conduct](https://thegooddocsproject.dev/) and the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

## Expected behavior

- Participate actively and authentically.
- Keep the commitments you make, and tell others early when you cannot.
- Attempt collaboration before conflict. Respect differing viewpoints and
  experience.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Show empathy and kindness. Be considerate in your word choice and actions.
- Accept responsibility, apologize to those affected by your mistakes, and
  learn from the experience.
- Focus on what is best for the overall community.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are unacceptable within any community space, online or offline,
public or private:

- Violent language or threats against another person.
- Discriminatory jokes and language.
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following.
- Personal insults, deliberate misgendering, name calling, or mockery.
- Trolling or sustained disruption of discussions or events.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies within all community spaces - the GitHub
repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official channel - and
when an individual represents the community in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at **conduct@kdeps.com**.
All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainers
respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, from a private written
warning through a temporary or permanent ban from the community, depending on
the severity and impact of the violation. Maintainers who do not follow or
enforce this code of conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent
repercussions as determined by other maintainers.
